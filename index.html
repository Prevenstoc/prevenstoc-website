How it works

Four steps, and the shelf never surprises you again

01

Receive stock

Scan the barcode, say the product name aloud, or import a Booker invoice — every delivery becomes its own tracked batch.

02

Get told what to check

Prevenstoc estimates expiry by category when a supplier doesn't provide one, then flags what needs checking today.

03

Sell with FIFO built in

Every sale deducts from the oldest batch automatically, so the stock that's ageing fastest sells first, without anyone thinking about it.

04

Audit in minutes

Count the shelf, log the variance, and see the shrinkage cost immediately — no spreadsheets, no end-of-week guesswork.

What you get

Everything a small shop needs, none of what it doesn't

Expiry intelligence

Category-based shelf-life estimates, escalating alerts, and a check schedule that tells staff exactly which shelf to look at today — not just a generic reminder.

Smart ordering

Reorder suggestions ranked by urgency and sales speed, with a budget cap that reprioritises the list automatically when money's tight this week.

Compliance built in

An age-verification log for alcohol, tobacco and vapes that replaces the paper register under the till — searchable, dated, and ready if a licensing officer asks.

Reports that mean something

Nine reports covering sales, margin, VAT, wastage and shrinkage — built from what's actually happened in your shop, not a template dashboard.
